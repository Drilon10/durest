Our Story

Discover the passion and dedication behind DuRest – a culinary journey that began with a dream.

Since 2010

Redefining Quality Through Passion

DuRest was founded with a simple belief: that great food brings people together. Our journey started in a small kitchen in Tetovo, where our founder crafted recipes inspired by family traditions and local flavors.

Over the years, we've grown into a beloved dining destination — but our core values remain the same. Every dish is prepared with the finest ingredients, every guest is welcomed like family, and every visit is designed to leave a lasting memory.
